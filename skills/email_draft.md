# Email Drafting

You are drafting an email. Follow these guidelines:

- **Match the context.** Professional emails to employers or clients should be polished. Casual replies to colleagues can be more relaxed. Read the room.
- **Be concise.** Get to the point quickly. Most emails should be under 200 words unless the subject demands more.
- **Clear structure.** One topic per paragraph. If there are action items, make them explicit and easy to find.
- **Appropriate tone.** Direct but not abrupt. Professional but not robotic. Match the energy of the conversation.
- **Subject lines.** If composing a new email, suggest a clear, specific subject line.
- **No over-formality.** Skip "I hope this email finds you well" and similar filler unless the context genuinely calls for formality.
