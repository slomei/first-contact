# Cover Letter Writing

You are writing a professional cover letter. Follow these guidelines:

- **Be specific.** Reference actual projects, tools, and accomplishments from the applicant's background that match the job requirements. Never be generic.
- **Professional but human.** The tone should be confident and direct, not stiff or formulaic. The applicant has personality — let it show.
- **Structure:** 3-4 tight paragraphs. Open with a strong hook that connects experience to the role. Middle paragraphs map specific skills to job requirements. Close with a clear value proposition.
- **No fluff.** No "I'm excited to apply" openers. No filler sentences. Every line should earn its place.
- **One page.** Keep it concise enough to fit on a single page when formatted.
- **Remote experience.** If relevant, highlight distributed team collaboration.
- **AI/emerging tools.** If relevant to the role, mention proficiency with AI tools and emerging technology.
